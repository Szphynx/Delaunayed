Getting started

Every device processes audio you give it — none of them make sound on their own. Feed one a loop or your microphone, then press play.

  1. Tap anywhere once. Browsers only let audio start inside a real interaction, so the first tap wakes the engine and loads the built-in loop.
  2. Choose a source. Load file takes WAV, AIFF, MP3 or M4A — you can also drag a file straight onto the page. Audio in uses your microphone instead. Loop repeats the loaded file rather than playing it once.
  3. Press ▶. The source starts and the device begins processing it.
  4. Set the mix. Dry/Wet decides how much processed sound you hear. The dry signal always passes underneath, so at 0% you hear the source untouched.

On the chassis devices, the canvas is the instrument and everything else lives on tabbed pages under it. The power square at the top left bypasses the device; the dice in the transport re-rolls every parameter, and the knob beside it sets how far a roll may throw.

Insert FX

Delaunay and Collapse both let you drop one effect onto a single tap or lane rather than the whole mix. Each has one knob.

EffectKnobSound
PhaserRateSweeping, hollow movement. Slow rates breathe; fast rates wobble.
Ring ModFreqMetallic and bell-like. Low frequencies growl, high ones turn pitched material into clangs.
GrainSizeA short smeared echo that thickens the voice. Small sizes shimmer, larger ones blur.
