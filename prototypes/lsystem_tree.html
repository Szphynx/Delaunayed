The rule

Those six numbers are the L-system: children per node, the turn between them, how much each generation shortens and quietens, how deep it goes.

Key

Wind

Wind bends the tips more than the trunk. Heading is pitch, so the tree retunes as it moves — the key snap makes that land as in-key steps, and the branch stretching drags its delay time, which is the chirp you hear on the way.
