What it is

The complete version of the constellation idea. Taps are triangulated across a plane; the playhead blends the three around it. The Voronoi dual of the same points gives hard-edged cells you can freeze and hold.

  1. Tap once to start audio, then load a file, drop one on the page, or use 🎤 Audio In.
  2. Press ▶, then drag the playhead around the field.
  3. Switch to Freeze · Voronoi when you want to hold cells rather than morph between taps.
  4. 💾 Save preset keeps a patch you like.

Voronoi freeze

In Freeze · Voronoi the plane is divided into cells, one per tap. Instead of blending, the device switches hard to whichever cell you are in and loops it.

Recipes

Morphing dub delay

Delay engine, Feedback around 60%, Tone rolled back, Auto-drift on. The tap blend keeps moving without you touching it.

Frozen chord stack

Freeze engine, Latch on, Freeze FB around 70%. Click three or four cells in turn to stack held loops, then Clear held to drop them.

Tempo-locked stutter

Freeze engine with ♪ Sync on, short freeze length, and drag across cells in time with the track.
