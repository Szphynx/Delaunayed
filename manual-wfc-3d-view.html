What it is

A visualizer rather than a full device: it runs the collapse and draws the result as a rotating block, so you can watch how a pattern resolves and how grid size changes its character.

Drag the scene to rotate it. Press ⧐ Collapse to solve a new pattern, or ↻ Roll to nudge the existing one.
