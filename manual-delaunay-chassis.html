What it is

Delay taps scattered across a plane. A playhead sits among them, and the three taps around it are blended by how close it is to each. Move the playhead and the delay morphs continuously.

Every dot is a full delay voice with its own time, pitch, level, feedback, stereo position and tone. Drag through the field and you glide between them; nothing switches abruptly.

  1. Tap anywhere once. Browsers only start audio inside a real interaction; the first tap wakes the engine and loads the built-in loop.
  2. Pick a source. Load file takes WAV, AIFF, MP3 or M4A, and you can drag a file onto the page. Audio in uses your microphone. Loop repeats the file instead of playing it once.
  3. Press ▶. The source starts and the device processes it.
  4. Set Dry/Wet on the MIX page. The dry signal always passes underneath, so at 0% you hear the source untouched.

Recipes

Navigable sweep

DLY. Glide around 200 ms, then drag the playhead slowly across the field — times and pitches morph as you cross each triangle. Switch on DRIFT to let it wander alone.

Freeze stutter

FRZ. Freeze len 60–150 ms, Freeze F/B around 50%. Hold and drag — each cell you cross latches a different loop.

Wide bloom

Width past 120%, Feedback around 60%, a few taps pitched up a fifth on the TAP page.
