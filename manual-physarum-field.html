Modulation Field.

The same colony, simpler job: its trail density modulates the feedback of a fixed eight-tap delay.

What it is

A free-running colony whose trail density under each of eight fixed taps sets how much that tap repeats. The delay network never rewires — only the amount of feedback moves — so this is the calmer, more predictable cousin of Physarum FDN.

Press ▶ Play and the field starts drifting. ↻ Reseed scatters the agents and lets a new pattern of density build.

Controls

ControlRangeWhat it does
Agents200–4000Colony size. More agents make denser, steadier modulation.
Speed4–25How fast the density pattern moves, and so how fast the taps swell and fade.
Sensitivity10–60How strongly agents follow existing trails. High settings hold shape longer.
Decay85–99Trail persistence. Low gives twitchy modulation, high gives slow swells.
Dry / Wet0–100%How much delay you hear over the source.
Feedback0–100%The ceiling the colony modulates under. The trails scale this, they do not exceed it.

Recipes

Slow breathing delay

Decay 97+, Speed low, Feedback around 70%. Taps swell in and out over several seconds.

Twitchy repeats

Decay near 85, Speed high, Agents low. Individual taps flicker in and out.
