What it is

A grid of steps across time and lanes of audio. A playhead sweeps the steps in tempo; wherever it meets a coloured tile, that lane fires. Painting the grid is how you write the rhythm.

  1. Tap anywhere once. Browsers only start audio inside a real interaction; the first tap wakes the engine and loads the built-in loop.
  2. Pick a source. Load file takes WAV, AIFF, MP3 or M4A, and you can drag a file onto the page. Audio in uses your microphone. Loop repeats the file instead of playing it once.
  3. Press ▶. The source starts and the device processes it.
  4. Set Dry/Wet on the MIX page. The dry signal always passes underneath, so at 0% you hear the source untouched.

Everything else lives on the six tabbed pages under the canvas. The power square at the top left bypasses the device; the dice in the transport re-rolls every parameter, and the knob beside it sets how far a roll may throw.

The tile alphabet

A tile is not an instrument — it is an envelope. It sets the shape of that hit, how loud it is, and how much of it feeds the echo bus.

Rest

Silence. The playhead passes without firing that lane.

Rise

Swells in, then drops. Longer than most tiles — good for pads and reverse-tape feels.

Sustain

Quick in, holds, then releases. The longest tile — it will overlap the next step and blur the grid.

Fall

Instant attack, quick decay. The tightest, most percussive tile.

Accent

Fall's shape at double the volume, with the strongest push into the echo. Use it on downbeats.

FB Junction

Sustain's shape, but nearly all of it goes to the echo bus instead of straight out. This is the tile that builds tails and self-sustaining loops.

The two engines

GRN and GATE are not a tone control. They are two different ways of turning your audio into sound, from the same painted grid.

Lanes have a fixed character. Lower lanes sit darker and further left, higher lanes brighter and further right. Choose which lane you paint on to place a sound in the mix.

Recipes

Rhythmic stutter

GRN. Short Grain (60–90 ms), Feedback low, mostly Fall and Accent tiles with rest between them. Raise Tempo for finer chatter.

Ambient wash

GRN. Long Grain (400 ms+), Sustain and Rise tiles filling the grid, Feedback high, Tone rolled back, Dry/Wet near 100%.

Gated echo groove

GATE. Step at 1/8 or 1/4, Fall tiles on a sparse pattern, Feedback 50–60%. Mute lanes on LVL while it plays.

Endless tail

Paint a few FB Junction tiles, push Feedback past 60% and lower Dry/Wet. The tails feed themselves and keep ringing after the source stops.
