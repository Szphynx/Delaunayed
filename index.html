Five unconventional systems

01

Delaunay Navigable Delay

Taps are points in a plane; the playhead's barycentric position inside its triangle crossfades the three vertex delay configs. A continuous, navigable morph-space of taps — this is the interactive build.

02

WFC Multitap

A Wave-Function-Collapse tap grid: adjacency constraints keep patterns coherent. Learn the rules from an example groove, then generate endless variations that still “belong.”

03

Reaction–Diffusion Spectral

Gray–Scott Turing patterns run on the spectrogram. Energy self-organizes and blooms across frequency & time — a freeze that grows, formant-like bands that migrate.

Listen — numpy sketch
04

Kuramoto Delay

A bank of coupled oscillators drives tap timing. Below threshold they drift; above it they spontaneously synchronize into an emergent pulse, then scatter. (Demo ramps coupling up.)

Listen — numpy sketch
05

Physarum FDN

Slime-mold agents grow a transport network that becomes the routing topology of a Feedback Delay Network — a reverb/space that reorganizes around the loudest frequencies.
